Every triangle drawn. Faces and edges take their plate's colour; seam faces belong to no plate and are rebuilt each step.

Simulated now: separate plate meshes on prescribed rigid rotations; per-column state; isostatic elevation; conductive thickening of L. Boundaries are classified each step from the signed gap and relative velocity. Gaps between plates are covered by seam faces rebuilt every step; when a seam grows wider than about one vertex spacing, both plates grow to its midline as new sea floor. Cracks within a single plate are filled outright. Orange is open mantle that nothing covers yet. Where plates overlap, the denser column subducts and is kept as slab (red overlay) until it is 933 km down-dip from the trench (drawn foreshortened to its 45° map footprint, fading with depth); buoyant columns that collide hand their crust to the overriding plate, and plates that keep colliding weld into one. Motions are still prescribed.
